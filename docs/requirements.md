# Requirements

## V1 Scope

- Single-user personal web app.
- Evaluate US and UK stocks from a watchlist.
- Show current or delayed price, daily move, status, confidence, factor scores, fair-value range, buy zone and sell zone.
- Show an explanation for each stock rating.
- Produce daily stock news summaries for watched stocks.
- Keep the system scalable for future accounts.
- Do not place trades in V1.

## Status Values

- Insane Cheap
- Buy
- Hold
- Sell
- Hard Sell
- Unrated

## Refresh Cadence

- Prices: target 15-minute refresh during market hours if provider limits allow.
- Fundamentals: daily or weekly.
- News: pre-market and post-market summaries.
- Full evaluation: after relevant data refreshes.

## Budget

Target operating cost is free for V1, with a hard preference to stay below 5 GBP per month.
